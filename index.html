On this device

Import from Files and Photos

Tap + and add titles from the Files app or pull videos out of Photos. Offly keeps audio and video in one library, with search across titles, people, and formats.

Resume where you left off. Favorites, Recently Added, In Progress, Lectures, and Lossless sit next to the playlists you make.

Linked folders

Your whole library, organised

Point Offly at a folder once. New files appear. Removed ones tidy up. Linked folders, including iCloud Drive, play in place so you are not duplicating a music collection onto the iPhone just to browse it.

Files on this device stay a separate room from Linked Folders. Import copies in. Linking leaves the originals where you put them.

Wi-Fi sharing

Beam files from a Mac or PC

Join the same Wi-Fi, start sharing in Offly, and open the address in Safari or Chrome. Scan the QR code if you would rather not type. Drop files onto the page. They show up on the iPhone.

The same local page can upload files, build playlists, and tidy the library from a laptop keyboard. Keep Offly open while you work. Guest networks usually block the link.

Music app

Downloads you already paid for

Offly can import songs that are already downloaded in the Music app on this device, then keep that list in sync. Albums, artists, and playlists come along. This is local playback of files on the phone, not Apple Music streaming.

Side B

Take it with you

CarPlay, Siri, and the Home Screen.

The same library follows you into the car and onto the lock screen. Hands on the wheel. Playback on the dash.

B1 · CarPlay

Your library in the car

Continue, Library, Playlists, and Favorites on CarPlay. Offly is built as a music app with a full in-car template, not a phone screen mirrored to the dash.

B2 · Siri

Just ask Siri

Say “Hey Siri, continue in Offly.” Play a playlist, play Favorites, Lectures, or In Progress, and set the equalizer from Shortcuts. No extra setup for the basic phrases.

B3 · Widgets

Control from the Home Screen

Cassette Pop widgets keep skip, play, and progress on the Home Screen. Pin a playlist and start it in one tap.

Voice & lock screen

Continue without unlocking

Offly registers as a player Siri already knows. If a phrase misses, ask Siri to add Offly shortcuts, or open them from the Shortcuts app. Background audio keeps lectures and albums going when the phone is in your pocket.

Sound

Playback

Tune the path from file to speaker.

Ten-band equalizer

31 Hz through 16 kHz, with presets for bass, voice, live rooms, rock, and a custom curve. Fine-tune every band, then save the shape you actually listen with.

Crossfade

Blend the end of one title into the next, from 1 to 10 seconds. Albums keep moving. Lectures do not slam into silence.

Sleep timer

Stop after 5, 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. Built for falling asleep to a katha, a film, or a long FLAC album.

HiFi, AirPlay, Picture in Picture

HiFi mode uses a music-oriented audio session. AirPlay sends sound to the room. Films can leave the player in Picture in Picture. Shuffle, repeat, and remembered volume sit in Settings.

FAQ

Before you download

What is an offline music player?

An offline music player plays files stored on the device, or in a folder you can reach without a network. Offly is a free offline music player for iPhone and iPad. You add the albums, lectures, and films you already own. Playback does not need a streaming account or a data connection.

Is Offly free?

Yes. Offly is free to use. Playback, import, linked folders, CarPlay, Siri, widgets, the equalizer, crossfade, and Wi-Fi transfer are there to play your own media. There is no streaming subscription and no account required to build a library.

Is this an offline MP3 and FLAC player for iPhone?

Yes. Offly plays local audio including MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, M4A, AIFF, CAF, and OGG. It also plays video in MP4, MOV, and M4V. It is a local file player, not a YouTube downloader and not an Apple Music streaming client.

How do I add music and films?

Import from the Files app or Photos, link a folder (including iCloud Drive) so Offly plays files in place, transfer over Wi-Fi from a Mac or PC browser, or bring in songs already downloaded in the Music app on this device.

Does Offly work without internet?

Playback is offline. Files on the device and files in a linked folder you can reach without a network keep playing on a plane, in a basement, or in the car. Wi-Fi sharing needs a local network only between the phone and the laptop, not the public internet.

Does Offly support CarPlay and Siri?

Yes. Offly has CarPlay for Continue, Library, Playlists, and Favorites. Siri phrases include “continue in Offly,” play a playlist, and play Favorites, Lectures, or In Progress. Home Screen widgets keep transport on the cassette.

Where do my files go?

Imported files live in Offly’s on-device library. Linked folders stay where you left them. Offly does not upload your library to a cloud of its own. Read the privacy policy for the full note.

What devices can run Offly?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 18 and iPadOS 18 or later. CarPlay uses the iPhone in the car. Wi-Fi sharing works with Safari or Chrome on a Mac or PC on the same network.
