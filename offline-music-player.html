Meaning

Local files

What an offline music player actually does

Streaming apps cache a catalog they still control. An offline music player is the other model. You keep the files. The app is there to organize them, remember your place, and play them when the network is gone.

Your library stays yours

Rips, purchases, lectures, and home video live in Offly as files. There is no Offly account and no Offly cloud. If you delete the app, imported copies leave with it. Linked folders stay where you put them.

Playback does not wait on Wi-Fi

Once a title is on the device, or in a linked folder you can reach without a network, Offly plays it. CarPlay, widgets, and Siri talk to that same local library.

Four doors

How music gets onto the iPhone

Import from the Files app or Photos. Those copies sit in On This Device.

Link a folder, including iCloud Drive. Offly plays those files where they are and does not duplicate the folder onto the phone just to list it.

Share via Wi-Fi from a Mac or PC on the same network. Open the local page in Safari or Chrome, or scan the QR code. Guest networks usually fail. Keep Offly open while you transfer.

Songs already downloaded in Apple’s Music app can join the library. That is local files on this device, not Apple Music streaming inside Offly.

Away from the desk

CarPlay, Siri, and the Home Screen

The same offline library shows up on CarPlay as Continue, Library, Playlists, and Favorites. Say “Hey Siri, continue in Offly” from the lock screen. Pin a cassette widget if you want transport without opening the app.

The ten-band equalizer, 1 to 10 second crossfade, and a 5 to 60 minute sleep timer stay with the file on the way to the speaker.
