Offly — fact sheet
Karma Academy

Name: Offly
One line: Free offline music and video player for iPhone and iPad.
Price: Free. Optional tips do not unlock playback.
Platforms: iPhone and iPad. iOS 18 / iPadOS 18.
Account: None required to build a library or play files.
Formats: MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, M4A, AIFF, CAF, OGG, MP4, MOV, M4V.
Library: Import from Files or Photos, or link a folder (including iCloud Drive) and play in place.
Transfer: Wi-Fi sharing from a Mac or PC browser. No cable.
CarPlay: Continue, Library, Playlists, Favorites.
Siri: Continue in Offly, play a playlist, play Favorites.
Widgets: Home Screen transport on the cassette player.
Sound: Ten-band equalizer, crossfade, sleep timer.
Privacy: No Offly cloud for your library.
