Offly Premium

Offly offers one optional in-app purchase through Apple: Offly Premium. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks Premium features, currently the parametric equalizer, AutoEq import and presets for each headphone or DAC, and any Premium features added later at no extra cost. Every feature that was free before Offly Premium existed stays free.

If you bought a tip or an Offly Support subscription before Offly Premium existed, Premium is already unlocked for your Apple Account. A refunded purchase no longer unlocks Premium. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the Offly Premium screen.

Purchases are billed by Apple to the Apple ID used on the device. Karma Academy does not receive your card details.

Offly Premium on Android

On Android, Offly Premium is a one-time purchase through Google Play, billed to the Google Account used on the device. Without it, Offly on Android holds up to three radio stations, three podcast shows, one music server, two smart playlists, and one linked folder. Premium lifts those limits and adds crossfade, the parametric equalizer, and online lyrics lookup. Restore Purchase is in Settings. Refunds are handled by Google under Google Play’s refund policies, and a refunded purchase no longer unlocks Premium.

Offly Premium is bought separately on each store. A purchase on the App Store does not unlock Premium on Android, and a purchase on Google Play does not unlock it on iPhone or iPad.

Earlier Offly Support subscriptions

Offly Support subscriptions are no longer sold. If you still have one from an earlier version, you can manage or cancel it in Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s refund rules.
